    About InactiveGuests

    InactiveGuests shows the dormant guest accounts in your Entra ID tenant so you can disable, delete or restore them in bulk.

    What it does

    • Lists every guest with creation date, redemption state, enabled state and last sign-in (interactive and non-interactive).
    • Filters on inactivity, account age, redemption state, enabled state and "never signed in".
    • Disables, enables or deletes the selection after showing you exactly who is affected.
    • Exports the filtered list to Excel or CSV.
    • Restores guests from the recycle bin within the 30 day window.

    How it works

    • Listing: GET /beta/users?$filter=userType eq 'Guest'&$select=...,signInActivity. signInActivity is reliable on beta only.
    • Inactivity: the most recent of lastSignInDateTime and lastNonInteractiveSignInDateTime. No sign-in at all falls back to createdDateTime, so a guest invited yesterday is not "inactive forever".
    • Disable and enable: PATCH /v1.0/users/{id} with accountEnabled.
    • Delete: DELETE /v1.0/users/{id}. The user stays restorable for 30 days.
    • Restore: POST /v1.0/directory/deletedItems/{id}/restore.

    Sign-in activity caveat

    signInActivity needs an Entra ID P1 or P2 licence in the tenant. Without it the field stays empty and inactivity is based on account age, which makes "never signed in" match everyone.
