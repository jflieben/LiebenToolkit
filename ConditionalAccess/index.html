    About ConditionalAccess

    ConditionalAccess exports, imports and compares Conditional Access policies, and gives a new tenant a basic starter set.

    What it does

    • Exports every policy to JSON, and the overview to Excel or CSV.
    • Imports policies from a file with an optional name prefix or suffix.
    • Lets you pick off, report-only or on per policy. The default is always report-only, whatever the file says.
    • Overwrites a policy with the exact same name only when you switch that on, and shows a field-by-field diff for each one first.
    • Shows every user, group, role, app, workload identity and named location a policy points at, with its name in your tenant, and makes you map the ones that do not exist.
    • Warns when a policy applies to all users with a block, or excludes nobody, and you are not excluded. One click adds you to the exclusions.
    • Compares two exports and shows added, removed and changed policies down to the field.

    How it works

    • Read: GET /v1.0/identity/conditionalAccess/policies and namedLocations.
    • Names: POST /v1.0/directoryObjects/getByIds, roleManagement/directory/roleDefinitions and servicePrincipals?$filter=appId in (...).
    • Lockout check: your own id plus GET /v1.0/me/transitiveMemberOf for groups and roles.
    • Write: POST /v1.0/identity/conditionalAccess/policies for new ones, PATCH .../policies/{id} for overwrites. One at a time.

    Before you switch anything on

    • Try it in a test tenant first.
    • Keep at least one emergency account excluded from every policy.
    • Let report-only run for a while and check the sign-in logs before going to on.
