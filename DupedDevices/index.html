Connect to your tenant

Sign in with the account you manage devices with. The tool reads Entra devices through Microsoft Graph, as you, and groups the ones that share a hardware id.

Delegated permissions asked at sign-in:

  • Device.Read.All list Entra devices
  • DeviceManagementManagedDevices.Read.All Intune primary user and Autopilot state (optional, the scan works without it)

Asked only when you first disable or delete: Directory.AccessAsUser.All. Graph has no narrower delegated permission for changing or deleting devices. The token can then do what your role allows, nothing more.

Roles: Cloud Device Administrator can disable and delete. Writing the timestamp to an extension attribute needs Intune Administrator.

      About DupedDevices

      DupedDevices finds devices that are registered more than once in Entra ID, keeps the most recent registration and lets you disable or delete the rest.

      How duplicates are found

      Every Entra device has a physicalIds list. DupedDevices takes the [HWID] entry (or the last part of [USER-HWID]) as the hardware fingerprint and groups devices that share it. In each group the device with the most recent approximateLastSignInDateTime (or createdDateTime) is kept; the others are stale candidates.

      Autopilot comes from Intune autopilotEnrolled when available, otherwise from the [ZTDId] marker in physicalIds.

      Only enabled devices are compared by default, so an old disabled record cannot shadow a fresh re-enrolment. Tick the checkbox on the Scan tab to include disabled devices.

      Cleanup workflow

      1. Optionally pick an extension attribute (1 to 15) for the disable timestamp and set a grace period.
      2. Scan and review the groups. Select the stale devices you want to clean up.
      3. Click Disable (recommended) or Delete. The confirm dialog lists every device that will change.
      4. Later, the Past grace period tab finds disabled devices whose timestamp is older than the grace period and deletes them after a fresh check.

      API calls

      • Devices: GET /v1.0/devices?$select=...,physicalIds,extensionAttributes.
      • Intune: GET /beta/deviceManagement/managedDevices?$select=azureADDeviceId,userPrincipalName,autopilotEnrolled,....
      • Disable: PATCH /v1.0/devices/{id} with accountEnabled: false and the optional timestamp.
      • Delete: DELETE /v1.0/devices/{id}. Deleted devices cannot be restored.
      • Grace period check: GET /v1.0/devices/{id} right before each delete.

      Permissions

      Reading needs Device.Read.All, plus DeviceManagementManagedDevices.Read.All for the Intune data. For changes Microsoft documents Directory.AccessAsUser.All as the only delegated permission for updating and deleting devices (Device.ReadWrite.All exists for app-only access). It is asked the first time you confirm a change.

      What it does not do

      • It does not touch Intune objects. Retire or wipe those in Intune.
      • It does not delete devices outside a duplicate group, except from the Past grace period tab.
      • The most recent device in a group can never be selected.
